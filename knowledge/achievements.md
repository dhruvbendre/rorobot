# Achievements

## Aavishkar Zonal Round winner (2025)

Dhruv Bendre won the Aavishkar Zonal Round in 2025 for developing a real-time sign language support system for video calls, built with WebRTC, MediaPipe, CVZone and a Random Forest classifier. The project was featured for the award.

## Published research

Dhruv's research on sign language support on video calls is published on Zenodo (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20352667). See the Research document for the full summary.
