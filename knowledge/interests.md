# Interests

Beyond work, Dhruv Bendre is curious about artificial intelligence, machine learning and data-driven solutions, and about how AI can change the way people work and interact with the world.

- Chess: he plays enough to have a personal PGN game database, which he used to train MyChessBot to play in his own style.
- Accessibility: his published research aims to make video calls usable for deaf and hard-of-hearing people.
- Making things: he describes a quiet itch to create, shaping and questioning an idea until every detail feels right.
- Cats: by his own admission, he will probably stop to say hello to a cat along the way.
