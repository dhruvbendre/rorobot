# Research

## Real-time sign language support on video calls (published on Zenodo)

Dhruv Bendre's research on sign language support for video calls is published on Zenodo with DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20352667 (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20352667).

The paper presents a real-time AI-powered sign language translation system designed to make video calls more accessible for deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals. The framework combines WebRTC, MediaPipe, CVZone and a Random Forest classifier to recognise sign language gestures, convert them into text and speech, and simultaneously transcribe spoken language into text for seamless two-way communication.

By using hand-landmark-based recognition instead of raw images, the system achieves 98.2% accuracy while remaining lightweight, robust to varying environments, and capable of low-latency performance on standard consumer hardware. It demonstrates the potential of AI to bridge communication barriers in everyday digital interactions.

The same project won the Aavishkar Zonal Round in 2025.
